# Phase 5 — Frontier (Year 3+)

## 1. Question

**Can the economy support livelihoods, not just learning?**

Phase 5 is the transition from a rewards product to a livelihood institution. It requires evidence that EC meaningfully covers essentials for real households, that governance is legitimate, and that the model can be opened without losing integrity.

**This phase is conditional.** It does not start unless Phase 4's audit, cost, and trust targets are met.

---

## 2. Scope

### In scope
- Livelihood pilots in 3 regions (5,000 participants).
- Open protocol for credentials and skills (25 partners).
- Autonomous content with human review (acceptance ≥70%).
- Learner-majority governance council.
- Annual independent economic audit.
- Public data and methodology.
- Policy engagement.

### Out of scope
- Universal basic income guarantees.
- Cash redemption of EC (unless legally structured).
- Replacing accredited education.
- Political advocacy beyond evidence-based policy input.

## 5. Livelihood pilot design

### 5.1 Eligibility
- Active learners with verified levels.
- Households in 3 regions.
- Mix of income levels.
- Consent to research.

### 5.2 Intervention
- Enhanced EC earning (sponsor-funded).
- Essentials catalog expansion.
- Mentoring and support.
- Savings tools.
- Financial coaching (optional, non-advisory).

### 5.3 Measurement
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Participants | 5,000 |
| Households covering ≥20% essentials | ≥60% |
| EC earned per month | Tracked |
| Essentials redeemed | Tracked |
| Financial stress (self-report) | Improved |
| Learning progression | Maintained |
| Dropout | <25% |
| Fraud loss | <1% |

### 5.4 Ethics
- Independent ethics review.
- No coercion to learn.
- No reduction of existing benefits (where applicable).
- Exit without penalty.
- Data protection.
- Community consultation.

---

## 6. Open protocol

### 6.1 What opens
- Credential schema and verification API.
- Skill taxonomy.
- Level outcome format.
- Assessment evidence format.
- Event schema for learning records.

### 6.2 What stays closed
- Item banks.
- Anti-fraud models.
- Learner data.
- Proprietary content.

### 6.3 Governance
- Open specification process.
- Partner advisory group.
- Compatibility certification.
- No pay-to-play.

---

## 7. Autonomous content

- AI drafts, human reviews.
- Acceptance rate ≥70% without rework.
- Safety and provenance mandatory.
- Learner feedback loop.
- Human sign-off remains for high-risk domains.
- Cost per level falls below $50.

---

## 8. Governance

### 8.1 Council
- Learner-majority seats.
- Sponsor seats (capped).
- Independent experts.
- Public minutes.
- Authority over: emission policy, marketplace rules, integrity standards, appeals at the top level.

### 8.2 Safeguards
- Charter and bylaws.
- Conflict-of-interest policy.
- Recall mechanism.
- Transparency report.
- External audit.

---

## 9. Policy engagement

- Publish methodology and outcomes.
- Brief governments on skills verification.
- Advise on AI-in-education standards.
- Contribute to open data.
- No lobbying for deregulation that harms learners.

---

## 10. Exit criteria

| Criterion | Target |
|---|---|
| Livelihood pilot participants | 5,000 |
| Households ≥20% essentials | ≥60% |
| Open protocol partners | 25 |
| Autonomous content acceptance | ≥70% |
| Governance council | Learner majority |
| Independent economic audit | Published |
| Fraud loss | <1% |
| Learning progression maintained | ≥90% of baseline |
| Ethics review | Clean |
| Scale decision | Documented |

### 10.1 Gate decision
- **Scale:** expand to more regions and sponsors.
- **Consolidate:** keep pilots, improve economics.
- **Stop:** evidence does not support livelihood claims; publish findings.

---

## 11. Requirements

- `FR-PHS-601`: Livelihood pilots require ethics review.
- `FR-PHS-602`: Participation is voluntary and exit is penalty-free.
- `FR-PHS-603`: Household outcomes are measured independently.
- `FR-PHS-604`: Open protocol is published with a compatibility process.
- `FR-PHS-605`: Autonomous content retains human review.
- `FR-PHS-606`: Governance has learner majority.
- `FR-PHS-607`: Economic audit is annual and public.
- `FR-PHS-608`: Policy claims cite published data.
- `FR-PHS-609`: Item banks and learner data stay protected.
- `FR-PHS-610`: Findings are published even if unfavorable.
- `NFR-PHS-601`: Public data portal updates monthly.
- `NFR-PHS-602`: Protocol API 99.9% availability.

---

## 12. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Livelihood claims overstated | Independent measurement; publish ranges |
| Pilot harms participants | Ethics review; exit rights; support |
| Protocol fragmentation | Certification; compatibility tests |
| Governance capture | Learner majority; transparency |
| Political backlash | Evidence-based framing; no overclaiming |
| Cost of pilots | Sponsor-funded; time-boxed |
