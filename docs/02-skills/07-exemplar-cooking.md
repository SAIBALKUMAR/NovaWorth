# Exemplar: Cooking & Nutrition — Basic Cooking Methods (COK-04)

## 1. Scope

This exemplar details the 100-level ladder for **`COK-04 Basic cooking methods`**, one of 25 skills in the Cooking & Nutrition domain. It shows how a physical, safety-sensitive craft is assessed without requiring a professional kitchen.

Related skills: `COK-01 Kitchen safety`, `COK-03 Food hygiene`, `COK-05 Meal planning`, `COK-16 Nutrition label reading`, `COK-23 Restaurant operations`, `COK-24 Food business`.

**Level 100 outcome:** Develops and validates cooking methods that work reliably at scale, and advances the craft through teaching, research, or product innovation.

## 2. The 10 bands

| Band | Levels | Identity | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–10 | Kitchen basics | Safety, hygiene, heat, and simple repeatable dishes |
| 2 | 11–20 | Everyday cook | Balanced meals, budgeting, batch cooking |
| 3 | 21–30 | Technique | Searing, braising, roasting, frying, seasoning |
| 4 | 31–40 | Cuisine & diet | Cultural fidelity, restrictions, nutrition |
| 5 | 41–50 | Diagnosis | Fixing failed dishes, timing, consistency |
| 6 | 51–60 | Professional | Service, costing, HACCP, menu design |
| 7 | 61–70 | Kitchen systems | Staffing, supply, quality control, scale |
| 8 | 71–80 | Product & R&D | Recipe development, shelf life, manufacturing |

## 3. Band detail

### Band 1 — Kitchen basics (1–10)
| Level | Can do | Evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Identify hazards, fire safety, knife safety, handwashing | Safety checklist + quiz |
| 2 | Hold and use a knife for safe chopping | Observed dice/mince |
| 3 | Prevent cross-contamination; store food safely | Kitchen audit |
| 4 | Boil, steam, and simmer without overcooking | Cook 3 vegetables to spec |
| 5 | Cook rice, pasta, and eggs consistently | Timed practical |
| 6 | Season to taste with salt, acid, fat, heat | Blind tasting |
| 7 | Make a simple pan sauce | Plate + rubric |

| 9 | Plan and cook a 2-course meal on time | Timed dinner |

## 4. Assessment design

| Band | Knowledge | Practical | Tasting/oral | Artifact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | 40% | 50% | 10% | 0% |
| 11–20 | 30% | 50% | 10% | 10% |
| 21–30 | 20% | 55% | 15% | 10% |
| 31–40 | 20% | 50% | 15% | 15% |
| 41–50 | 15% | 50% | 20% | 15% |
| 51–60 | 10% | 55% | 15% | 20% |
| 61–70 | 10% | 40% | 20% | 30% |
| 71–80 | 10% | 30% | 20% | 40% |
| 81–90 | 5% | 20% | 30% | 45% |
| 91–100 | 0% | 10% | 40% | 50% |

### 4.1 Practical rubric
| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Safety & hygiene | Unsafe | Needs reminders | Safe | Models best practice |
| Technique | Major errors | Inconsistent | Correct | Precise and efficient |
| Seasoning & balance | Unbalanced | Basic | Balanced | Nuanced |
| Timing & workflow | Chaotic | Rushed | On time | Calm and ahead |

### 4.2 Evidence
- Photos/video of the process, not just the plate.
- Temperature logs where relevant.
- Timed service recordings.
- Cost sheets and menus.
- Tasting panel scores.

---

## 5. Safety and scope

- High-risk: knife work, heat, oil, pressure, fermentation, allergens.
- Learners must confirm safety briefs before practicals.
- No level claims professional certification or food-safety licensure.
- Allergen content is mandatory at every level.
- Fermentation/canning levels require pH/testing evidence.
- Minors require guardian consent for heat/oil practicals.

---

## 6. Anti-gaming

- Video evidence must show the learner's hands and workspace.
- Random ingredient substitutions at high levels.
- Tasting panels are blinded.
- Cost sheets are checked against receipts.
- Plagiarized recipes are detected by provenance and variation.
- Service sims use randomized disruptions.

---

## 7. Post-100 paths

- Constellation: zero-waste restaurant (COK + BIZ + HOM + MED).
- Product R&D for a food company.
- Teaching and curriculum design.
- Food policy and school nutrition.
- Research in sensory science or preservation.
