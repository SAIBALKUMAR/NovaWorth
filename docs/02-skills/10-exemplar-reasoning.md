# Exemplar: Reasoning — Critical Thinking (LRN-17)

## 1. Scope

This exemplar details the 100-level ladder for **`LRN-17 Critical thinking`**, a meta-skill that amplifies every other domain. It shows how to assess transfer, bias awareness, argument quality, and decision-making under uncertainty.

Related skills: `MTH-11 Logic & proofs`, `MTH-19 Risk & uncertainty`, `LRN-18 Bias awareness`, `LRN-19 Decision making`, `SCI-19 Evidence evaluation`.

**Level 100 outcome:** Advances the field of reasoning through original research, methods, or institutions that improve how people think under uncertainty.

---

## 2. The 10 bands

| Band | Levels | Identity | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–10 | Awareness | Claims, evidence, assumptions, fallacies |
| 2 | 11–20 | Analysis | Arguments, sources, base rates, correlation |
| 3 | 21–30 | Everyday reasoning | Decisions, news, money, health claims |
| 4 | 31–40 | Formal tools | Logic, probability, causal diagrams |
| 5 | 41–50 | Uncertainty | Calibration, forecasting, decision trees |
| 6 | 51–60 | Research | Evidence synthesis, study design, meta-reasoning |
| 7 | 61–70 | Teaching | Teaching reasoning, assessing it |
| 8 | 71–80 | Systems | Institutional decision quality, red teams |
| 9 | 81–90 | Frontier | Novel problems, adversarial information, policy |
| 10 | 91–100 | World-class | Research, methods, field leadership |

|---:|---|---|

| 91–100 | 0% | 5% | 40% | 55% |

### 4.1 Rubric
| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logic | Invalid | Weak | Sound | Rigorous |
| Evidence | Irrelevant | Thin | Appropriate | Decisive |
| Uncertainty | Ignored | Acknowledged | Quantified | Well-calibrated |
| Intellectual honesty | Biased | Inconsistent | Fair | Exemplary |

### 4.2 Transfer tasks
- Apply a reasoning tool to a domain the learner has not studied.
- Evaluate a claim with missing data.
- Update a belief after disconfirming evidence.
- Explain a complex argument to a novice.
- Detect a subtle flaw in an expert's reasoning.

---

## 5. Safety and ethics

- No content that encourages manipulation or coercion.
- Distinguish reasoning from rhetoric.
- Respect lived experience and cultural context.
- Avoid pseudo-profundity and "just asking questions" harassment.
- Escalation guidance for mental health or crisis content.

---

## 6. Anti-gaming

- Novel prompts and randomized cases.
- Oral defense of written arguments.
- Process evidence (drafts, forecasts, updates).
- AI-generated arguments are detected and must be declared.
- Calibration is measured over time, not one lucky prediction.

---

## 7. Post-100 paths

- Constellation: crisis newsroom (LRN + MED + CIV + AID).
- Research in judgment and decision-making.
- Policy advisory.
- Building a forecasting or red-team institution.
- Teaching reasoning at scale.
