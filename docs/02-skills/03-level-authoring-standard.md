# Level Authoring Standard

## 1. Purpose

This is the contract for every one of the 50,000 level specifications. A level is not a video, a chapter, or a quiz. It is a **bounded capability** with a clear outcome, practice, evidence, and a gate.

If a level cannot be assessed, it is not a level. If it cannot be learned, it is not a level. If it does not change what the learner can do, it is not a level.

---

## 2. The 12 required fields

Every level spec must contain all 12 fields. Missing fields block publication.

| # | Field | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Level ID & title** | `CMP-06-L42`; short, concrete title |
| 2 | **Outcome** | "After this level, you can ..." — observable, testable |
| 3 | **Prerequisites** | Prior levels, co-requisites, tools, safety clearance |
| 4 | **Why it matters** | Real consequence; motivation without hype |
| 5 | **Learn** | 2–5 concepts with explanation, example, counterexample |
| 6 | **Practice** | 3–8 exercises, including ≥1 applied task |
| 7 | **Common mistakes** | Misconceptions, detection, remediation |
| 8 | **Assessment blueprint** | Item mix, practical task, rubric, pass criteria |
| 9 | **Evidence** | What the learner submits and how it is verified |
| 10 | **Time & difficulty** | Expected hours, D index, prerequisite load |
| 11 | **Rewards** | XP, EC, band bonus eligibility |
| 12 | **Safety & ethics** | Risks, warnings, scope limits, escalation |

## 3. Outcome statements

### 3.1 Formula
> After this level, you can **[observable action]** **[object/context]** **[quality standard]**.

### 3.2 Good vs bad

| Bad | Why | Good |
|---|---|---|
| "Understand loops" | Not observable | "Write a loop that processes a list and handles empty input correctly" |
| "Learn about nutrition" | Vague | "Build a 3-day meal plan that meets a stated calorie and protein target" |
| "Be good at communication" | Unmeasurable | "Deliver a 2-minute update that states the decision, evidence, and ask" |
| "Master Kubernetes" | Tool-centric, unbounded | "Deploy a 3-service app with health checks and rollback" |
| "Know first aid" | Unsafe ambiguity | "Perform CPR and AED use on an adult manikin to current guideline standard" |

### 3.3 Quality bar
- Uses an action verb from Bloom's taxonomy (apply, analyze, evaluate, create).
- Names the context and constraints.
- Has a pass standard a stranger could apply.
- Is achievable in the expected hours.
- Does not depend on a single proprietary tool.

## 5. Content volume per level

| Component | Minimum | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Concepts | 2 | 3–5 |
| Worked examples | 1 | 2–3 |
| Practice exercises | 3 | 5–8 |
| Applied task | 1 | 1–2 |
| Common mistakes | 2 | 3–5 |
| Assessment items | 10 | 20–40 in bank |
| Practical rubric criteria | 3 | 4 |
| Estimated authoring time | 4h | 8–12h |

A level with fewer than 3 practice exercises or no applied task is rejected.

## 6. Assessment blueprint

### 6.1 By band
| Band | Knowledge | Applied | Transfer/oral | Artifact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | 70% | 30% | 0% | 0% |
| 11–20 | 55% | 35% | 10% | 0% |
| 21–30 | 40% | 40% | 10% | 10% |
| 31–40 | 30% | 40% | 10% | 20% |
| 41–50 | 25% | 35% | 15% | 25% |
| 51–60 | 20% | 30% | 20% | 30% |
| 61–70 | 15% | 25% | 25% | 35% |
| 71–80 | 10% | 20% | 30% | 40% |
| 81–90 | 5% | 15% | 35% | 45% |
| 91–100 | 0% | 10% | 40% | 50% |

### 6.2 Pass criteria
- Knowledge: ≥80%; distinction ≥95%.
- Applied: rubric average ≥2.5/4; no criterion <2.
- Transfer/oral: ≥3/4 on explanation and transfer.
- Artifact: ≥3/4 on all critical criteria; expert panel at 90+.

### 6.3 Retakes
- Formative: unlimited.
- Summative: 3 attempts per 24h; 7-day cooldown after 3 failures.
- A retake uses a different item set.
- Retake fees (EC) may apply at high levels; never required to pass.

## 7. Evidence standards

| Evidence type | Strength | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple-choice quiz | Low | Auto-graded, randomized |
| Short answer | Medium | Rubric + AI + sample |
| Code artifact | High | Tests, review, provenance |
| Document/plan | High | Rubric + human sample |
| Video performance | High | Rubric + expert |
| Live simulation | High | Logs + outcome + review |
| Real-world impact | Highest | Partner verification + evidence |
| Oral defense | High | Live panel |

### 7.1 Evidence rules
- Evidence must be attributable to the learner.
- Evidence must be dated and versioned.
- AI assistance must be declared where allowed.
- Evidence must be retained for the credential lifetime (or as required by law).
- Learners can export their evidence.

## 8. Safety and ethics

### 8.1 Safety classification
| Class | Examples | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| `standard` | Writing, coding | Normal review |
| `caution` | Cooking, exercise, finance | Warnings, scope limits |
| `high-risk` | First aid, electrical, climbing, mental health crisis | Expert review, simulation-first, explicit "not a license" |

### 8.2 Required for high-risk levels
- A safety brief before practice.
- A supervised or simulated alternative.
- Clear scope: what the learner may and may not do.
- Escalation guidance.
- Legal review by region.
- No level may claim professional licensure.

### 8.3 Ethics
- No content that encourages harm, coercion, or exploitation.
- No medical/legal/financial advice presented as professional service.
- No dark patterns.
- Respect cultural context and consent.

---

## 9. Localization

- Outcomes are locale-neutral unless the skill is jurisdiction-specific.
- Examples use local units, currency, and context.
- Assessments are culturally reviewed.
- Language level is controlled (B1–B2 for early bands).
- Captions/transcripts for all media.
- Right-to-left and CJK layout tested.

---

## 10. Versioning

- Each level has `version` (semver) and `effective_from`.
- Changes are classified: patch (typo), minor (example), major (outcome/assessment).
- Major changes require re-validation and learner notice.
- Historical attempts keep the version they used.
- Credentials record the level version.

---

## 11. Review checklist

A level cannot publish until all are true:

- [ ] 12 fields complete.
- [ ] Outcome is observable and testable.
- [ ] Prerequisites are correct and available.
- [ ] At least 3 practice exercises, including 1 applied.
- [ ] Assessment blueprint matches the band.
- [ ] Rubric has 4 criteria and 4 performance levels.
- [ ] Item bank has ≥20 items (or a justified equivalent).
- [ ] Common mistakes are addressed.
- [ ] Safety/ethics review complete.
- [ ] Accessibility review complete.
- [ ] Localization notes complete.
- [ ] Expert reviewer signed off.
- [ ] Pilot pass rate within target range.
- [ ] Time estimate validated with ≥10 learners.
- [ ] Evidence retention and export configured.

- `FR-SKL-203`: Every level has an applied task.
- `FR-SKL-204`: Every assessment blueprint matches the band.
- `FR-SKL-205`: Every high-risk level has a safety review.
- `FR-SKL-206`: Every level has a version and changelog.
- `FR-SKL-207`: Every level is accessible and localized.
- `FR-SKL-208`: Every level has a pilot before full release.
- `NFR-SKL-201`: Level page loads in <300 ms.
- `NFR-SKL-202`: Level content works offline on mobile.
