# Exemplar: Health & Body — First Aid (HLT-10)

## 1. Scope

This exemplar details the 100-level ladder for **`HLT-10 First aid`**, a safety-critical skill. It shows how the product handles high-risk content: simulation-first, scope-limited, expert-reviewed, and never a substitute for certification.

Related skills: `HLT-09 Preventive health`, `HLT-11 CPR & AED`, `HLT-21 Emergency preparedness`, `MND-20 Crisis support`, `OUT-10 Wilderness first aid`.

**Level 100 outcome:** Leads emergency-response education and system design, with verified impact on survival, training, or policy.

> **Safety notice:** NovaWorth credentials are not medical licenses. Learners must follow local law and seek professional care. High-risk practicals require supervised or simulated settings.

## 2. The 10 bands

| Band | Levels | Identity | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | 11–20 | Basic response | Bleeding, burns, choking, shock, recovery position |
| 3 | 21–30 | CPR & AED | High-quality CPR, AED, team response |
| 4 | 31–40 | Assessment | Scene safety, primary survey, vital signs, handover |
| 5 | 41–50 | Complex scenes | Multiple casualties, environmental, chronic conditions |
| 6 | 51–60 | Team leadership | Incident command, triage, communication |
| 7 | 61–70 | Training | Teach first aid, run drills, assess competence |
| 8 | 71–80 | Systems | Design response plans, kits, protocols |
| 9 | 81–90 | Community | Build resilience programs, mass-casualty drills |
| 10 | 91–100 | World-class | Research, policy, and field leadership |

### 4.1 Rubric
| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Safety | Unsafe | Risky | Safe | Anticipates risk |
| Sequence | Wrong order | Misses steps | Correct | Adaptive |
| Communication | Unclear | Basic | Clear | Calm and reassuring |
| Judgment | Poor | Rule-bound | Sound | Excellent under ambiguity |

### 4.2 Mandatory safety
- Scene safety before care.
- Consent where possible.
- Infection control.
- Scope limits: no diagnosis, no medication beyond training, no invasive procedures.
- Escalation to emergency services.
- Debrief after every high-stress sim.

---

## 5. Anti-gaming

- Manikin/feedback devices for CPR quality.
- Randomized scenarios.
- Assessor observation, not self-report.
- Video review for team drills.
- No credit for watching; only demonstrated performance.
- High-stakes levels require in-person or supervised simulation.
