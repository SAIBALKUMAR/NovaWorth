## Coverage notes

- 20 domains × 25 skills = 500. Every domain has the same shape for navigation and funding.
- 100 levels × 500 skills = 50,000 level specifications. The five exemplars show the required depth; the remaining 495 skills are authored through the content pipeline.
- Skills are not ranked against each other. Domain multipliers reflect scarcity and risk, not human worth.
- The catalog is versioned. Adding/removing a skill requires a change proposal, expert review, and learner notice.
