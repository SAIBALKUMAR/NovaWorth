# Exemplar: Communication — Active Listening (COM-01)

## 1. Scope

This exemplar details the 100-level ladder for **`COM-01 Active listening`**, a relational skill. It shows how soft skills are assessed credibly through role-play, oral defense, reflective artifacts, and third-party outcomes — without reducing them to a personality quiz.

Related skills: `COM-06 Conflict resolution`, `COM-10 Difficult conversations`, `COM-21 Teaching & explanation`, `MND-24 Couples communication`, `WRK-12 Giving & receiving feedback`.

**Level 100 outcome:** Coaches leaders in high-stakes listening and resolves entrenched conflicts through deep inquiry, with verified impact on people and systems.

### 4.1 Rubric
| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Presence | Distracted | Inconsistent | Attentive | Deeply present |
| Accuracy | Misunderstands | Partial | Accurate | Nuanced |
| Empathy | Cold | Formulaic | Warm | Validating and precise |
| Judgment | Advises poorly | Generic | Appropriate | Excellent boundaries |

### 4.2 Evidence rules
- Recorded role-plays with consent.
- Trained actors, not friends, for high-stakes gates.
- Third-party feedback is verified and specific.
- Reflective artifacts are checked for specificity, not performative vulnerability.
- AI analysis is allowed but human-scored at high levels.

---

## 5. Safety and ethics

- Learners must not practice on real people in crisis without supervision.
- Scope limits: active listening is not therapy.
- Escalation guidance for suicide, abuse, and medical emergencies.
- Consent for recording; secure storage; deletion on request.
- Cultural humility: no assumption that one listening style fits all.

---

## 6. Anti-gaming

- Actors are trained to detect scripted empathy.
- Scenarios are randomized.
- Learners must respond to unexpected disclosures.
- Third-party feedback is verified.
- AI-generated reflections are checked against recordings.
- High-stakes gates use live assessors.
