**HOM-08 · Electrical safety.** Working safely around electricity within scope. From changing a fixture to working safely around electricity within scope.

**HOM-13 · Pest management.** Controlling pests safely and preventively. From sealing entry points to managing pests safely and preventively.

**HOM-14 · Home security.** Protecting a home in layered ways. From locks and light to designing layered home security.

**HOM-23 · Smart home.** Automating a home reliably and privately. From a smart bulb to designing reliable, private smart-home systems.

**HOM-24 · Renovation project management.** Running a renovation on time and budget. From a small room to running a renovation on budget and schedule.

**HOM-25 · Sustainable home systems.** Designing homes that use less energy and water. From simple savings to designing low-energy, low-water homes.
