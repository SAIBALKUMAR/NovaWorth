## AI & Data (AID)

**AID-01 · AI literacy.** Knowing what AI can and cannot do — and saying so clearly. From basic concepts to explaining AI capabilities and limits to executives and the public.

**AID-02 · Prompt engineering.** Directing a model to produce reliable, evaluated output. From simple prompts to designing robust prompts and evaluation suites for production AI.

**AID-03 · Data collection & cleaning.** Gathering and fixing the data models learn from. From tidy datasets to building data pipelines that meet production quality standards.

**AID-04 · Exploratory data analysis.** Finding the signal inside messy data before modeling. From summary stats to finding decisive insights in messy data quickly.

**AID-05 · Statistics for ML.** The statistical discipline underneath machine learning. From distributions to applying statistical rigor to model development and evaluation.

**AID-06 · Machine learning.** Systems that learn patterns and make predictions. From a first model to building and deploying ML systems with measured impact.

**AID-07 · Deep learning.** Neural networks that learn rich representations. From a first network to designing and training deep models for novel problems.

**AID-08 · Natural language processing.** Teaching machines to read and write language. From text basics to building NLP systems at frontier quality.

**AID-09 · Computer vision.** Teaching machines to see. From image basics to deploying vision systems in safety-critical environments.

**AID-10 · Speech & audio AI.** Machines that hear and speak. From basic recognition to building robust speech systems across accents and noise.

**AID-11 · Recommender systems.** Matching people to things they want. From basic ranking to designing recommenders that balance relevance and wellbeing.

**AID-12 · LLM application design.** Building products on large language models. From a first app to shipping reliable LLM products with strong evaluations.

**AID-13 · Retrieval-augmented generation.** Grounding generation in retrieved facts. From simple lookups to designing retrieval systems with high factual accuracy.

**AID-14 · Agentic workflows.** Systems that plan and act across tools. From a single agent to building multi-agent systems that complete real work safely.

**AID-15 · Model evaluation.** Measuring whether a model is actually good. From a metric to designing evaluation programs that catch regressions and bias.

**AID-16 · AI safety & alignment.** Keeping AI systems aligned and harmless. From basic principles to leading AI safety reviews and red-teaming programs.

**AID-17 · MLOps.** Operating machine learning in production. From a training script to running ML platforms with reproducible training and deployment.

**AID-18 · Data engineering.** Moving and storing data at scale. From a basic pipeline to building petabyte-scale data platforms.

**AID-19 · Analytics engineering.** Turning raw data into trusted metrics. From a dashboard to building trusted analytics layers used for decisions.

**AID-20 · Visualization & dashboards.** Communicating data visually with effect. From a chart to designing visualizations that change decisions.

**AID-21 · Causal inference.** Distinguishing cause from correlation. From basic intuition to estimating causal effects rigorously in observational data.

**AID-22 · AI product management.** Shaping AI products people actually adopt. From a feature to leading AI products from discovery to scaled adoption.

**AID-23 · Synthetic data.** Generating data when real data is scarce or sensitive. From basic generation to producing synthetic data with validated utility and privacy.

**AID-24 · Edge AI.** Running intelligence on small, local devices. From a tiny model to deploying AI under latency, power, and privacy constraints.

**AID-25 · AI governance.** Rules and oversight for responsible AI. From policy basics to designing AI governance frameworks adopted by institutions.
