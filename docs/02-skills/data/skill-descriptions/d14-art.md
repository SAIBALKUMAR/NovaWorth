## Arts & Design (ART)

**ART-01 · Visual literacy.** Reading images and visual culture with nuance. From noticing to reading visual culture with expert nuance.

**ART-02 · Drawing fundamentals.** Drawing from observation and imagination. From a first sketch to drawing confidently from observation and imagination.

**ART-03 · Color theory.** Using color with knowledge and control. From the wheel to using color with expert control.

**ART-04 · Composition.** Arranging a frame so it holds the eye. From balance to composing images that hold attention.

**ART-05 · Photography.** Making pictures worth publishing. From a snapshot to producing photographs at publication quality.

**ART-06 · Graphic design.** Designing identities and systems clearly. From a poster to designing identities and systems at professional level.

**ART-07 · Typography.** Setting type that reads and feels right. From a font to setting type at expert level.

**ART-08 · UX research.** Learning what users need and why. From a question to running research that changes product decisions.

**ART-09 · UX/UI design.** Designing products everyone can use. From a screen to designing accessible products at scale.

**ART-10 · Design systems.** Building reusable design language. From a component to building design systems used by large teams.

**ART-11 · Illustration.** Drawing images that communicate. From a doodle to illustrating at publication quality.

**ART-12 · 3D modeling.** Building digital objects for any purpose. From a shape to modeling production-ready 3D assets.

**ART-13 · Animation.** Bringing images to life with motion. From a bouncing ball to animating at studio quality.

**ART-14 · Video editing.** Cutting footage into a story that holds. From a trim to editing video that holds attention.

**ART-15 · Sound design.** Crafting audio that shapes experience. From an effect to designing sound for immersive media.

**ART-16 · Creative writing.** Writing prose of publishable quality. From a story to writing fiction or nonfiction of publishable quality.

**ART-17 · Poetry.** Making language carry more than it says. From a first poem to writing poetry of publishable quality.

**ART-18 · Screenwriting.** Writing stories for the screen. From a scene to writing screenplays at professional standard.

**ART-19 · Game design.** Designing games with deep, fair systems. From a rule to designing games with deep, fair systems.

**ART-20 · Fashion design.** Designing clothing with art and commerce. From a garment to designing collections with commercial and artistic merit.

**ART-21 · Industrial design.** Designing products that are beautiful and buildable. From a concept to designing products that are beautiful and manufacturable.

**ART-22 · Architecture literacy.** Reading buildings and spaces with expertise. From observing to reading and critiquing architecture with expertise.

**ART-23 · Exhibition design.** Designing spaces that move visitors. From a layout to designing exhibitions that move visitors.

**ART-24 · Creative direction.** Leading the visual voice of a brand. From a mood board to leading creative direction for major brands.

**ART-25 · Portfolio & client work.** Winning and delivering high-value creative work. From a first piece to winning and delivering high-value creative work.
