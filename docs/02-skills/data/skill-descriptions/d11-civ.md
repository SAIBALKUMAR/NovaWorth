## Civics & Law (CIV)

**CIV-02 · Voting & elections.** Participating in elections with real information. From registering to navigating elections and civic information.

**CIV-04 · Legal literacy.** Reading legal language with a critical eye. From everyday terms to reading contracts and legal documents critically.

**CIV-05 · Contracts.** Understanding and shaping agreements. From a simple agreement to drafting and negotiating clear contracts.

**CIV-06 · Consumer law.** Using the law to resolve unfair treatment. From a complaint to using consumer law to resolve disputes.

**CIV-09 · Family law basics.** Understanding the legal shape of family life. From key processes to understanding family law processes.

**CIV-12 · Data rights.** Controlling your personal data across systems. From privacy basics to exercising data rights across jurisdictions.

**CIV-19 · Emergency services.** Accessing and coordinating help in a crisis. From dialing to accessing and coordinating emergency services.

**CIV-21 · Disability rights.** Advocating for access and accommodations. From the basics to advocating for disability rights and accommodations.
