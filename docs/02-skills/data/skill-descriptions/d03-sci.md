## Science & Systems Thinking (SCI)

**SCI-01 · Scientific method.** The craft of asking questions nature can answer. From posing a testable question to designing rigorous investigations and teaching scientific reasoning.

**SCI-02 · Physics fundamentals.** How matter, motion, and energy actually behave. From everyday mechanics to explaining everyday and frontier physics with quantitative accuracy.

**SCI-03 · Chemistry fundamentals.** How substances form, react, and transform. From basic reactions to applying chemistry safely to novel materials and reactions.

**SCI-04 · Biology fundamentals.** The logic of living systems. From cells to ecosystems, mastery reasons across biological systems from molecules to whole ecosystems.

**SCI-05 · Earth & climate science.** How the planet works and is changing. From reading weather to interpreting climate evidence and designing resilience strategies.

**SCI-06 · Astronomy.** The universe beyond Earth, read through data. From recognizing the sky to analyzing astronomical data and communicating frontier findings.

**SCI-07 · Ecology.** How organisms and environments shape each other. From basic food webs to designing ecosystem restoration plans with measurable outcomes.

**SCI-08 · Human anatomy.** The structure of the human body. From naming parts to applying anatomy in clinical or performance contexts with expert accuracy.

**SCI-09 · Genetics.** How traits and information flow across generations. From basic inheritance to interpreting genomic data and explaining inheritance at a clinical level.

**SCI-10 · Neuroscience basics.** How brains produce mind and behavior. From basic wiring to connecting neuroscience evidence to learning and behavior design.

**SCI-11 · Systems thinking.** Seeing wholes, feedback, and interdependence. From basic maps to mapping complex systems and identifying high-leverage interventions.

**SCI-12 · Feedback loops.** The cycles that stabilize or destabilize systems. From recognizing a loop to diagnosing reinforcing and balancing loops in organizations and ecosystems.

**SCI-13 · Complexity & emergence.** How simple rules produce surprising wholes. From noticing emergence to modeling emergent behavior and advising on complex adaptive systems.

**SCI-14 · Energy systems.** How energy is generated, moved, and used. From basic principles to designing or evaluating energy systems at grid scale.

**SCI-15 · Materials science.** Why materials behave — and how to choose them. From common materials to selecting and inventing materials for demanding applications.

**SCI-16 · Engineering design.** Turning constraints into working artifacts. From a first prototype to leading engineering design from concept to verified deployment.

**SCI-17 · Experimentation & lab safety.** Running experiments that are both rigorous and safe. From basic safety to running safe labs and training others in experimental rigor.

**SCI-18 · Science communication.** Translating science for public decisions without distortion. From clear explanations to translating frontier science accurately for policy and the public.

**SCI-19 · Evidence evaluation.** Judging how much a claim is actually supported. From basic skepticism to evaluating evidence quality across disciplines and setting standards.

**SCI-20 · Epidemiology.** How disease moves through populations. From basic patterns to interpreting and designing epidemiological studies at public-health level.

**SCI-21 · Nutrition science.** What food actually does to a body, on evidence. From basic needs to critiquing nutrition evidence and designing evidence-based programs.

**SCI-22 · Pharmacology basics.** How drugs act and interact. From common medicines to understanding drug mechanisms and interactions at clinical-literacy level.

**SCI-23 · Robotics fundamentals.** Building machines that sense and act. From basic components to designing and debugging robotic systems in unstructured environments.

**SCI-24 · Space systems.** Engineering that survives the vacuum. From orbital basics to engineering space systems under extreme constraints.

**SCI-25 · Research synthesis.** Combining many studies into one trustworthy answer. From reading papers to producing authoritative systematic reviews that shape fields.
