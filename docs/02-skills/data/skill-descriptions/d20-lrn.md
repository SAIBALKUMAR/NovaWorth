## Learning & Meta-Skills (LRN)

**LRN-01 · Learning how to learn.** Designing your own learning system. From a habit to designing personal learning systems.

**LRN-02 · Goal setting.** Setting goals that survive contact with reality. From a wish to setting goals that survive reality.

**LRN-03 · Deliberate practice.** Practicing at the edge of your ability, on purpose. From repetition to running deliberate practice.

**LRN-04 · Retrieval practice.** Recalling to strengthen memory. From rereading to using retrieval practice effectively.

**LRN-05 · Spaced repetition.** Scheduling review so it sticks. From a flashcard to building spaced repetition systems.

**LRN-06 · Note-taking.** Taking notes that compound into understanding. From scribbles to taking notes that compound.

**LRN-07 · Reading comprehension.** Reading complex texts deeply. From the gist to reading complex texts deeply.

**LRN-08 · Speed reading.** Reading faster without losing meaning. From pacing to reading quickly with retention.

**LRN-09 · Memory techniques.** Encoding information so it stays. From a mnemonic to using memory techniques.

**LRN-10 · Focus & deep work.** Sustaining concentration on hard problems. From a session to sustaining deep work.

**LRN-11 · Metacognition.** Thinking accurately about your own thinking. From awareness to thinking about thinking accurately.

**LRN-12 · Self-assessment.** Judging your own competence honestly. From guesswork to assessing own competence honestly.

**LRN-13 · Feedback literacy.** Using feedback to actually improve. From defensiveness to using feedback to improve.

**LRN-14 · Transfer of learning.** Applying learning across domains. From one case to transferring learning across domains.

**LRN-15 · Curiosity.** Sustaining the drive to explore. From a question to sustaining curiosity.

**LRN-16 · Research skills.** Researching to an expert standard. From a search to researching to expert standard.

**LRN-17 · Critical thinking.** Thinking critically under pressure. From a claim to thinking critically under pressure.

**LRN-18 · Bias awareness.** Detecting bias in yourself and systems. From a blind spot to detecting bias in self and systems.

**LRN-19 · Decision making.** Deciding well under uncertainty. From a choice to making decisions under uncertainty.

**LRN-20 · Systems mapping.** Mapping how a system really works. From a diagram to mapping systems.

**LRN-21 · Creative problem solving.** Solving problems with originality. From a brainstorm to solving problems creatively.

**LRN-22 · Experimentation.** Running small tests to learn fast. From a trial to running personal experiments.

**LRN-23 · Reflection.** Reflecting in ways that change behavior. From a review to reflecting in ways that change behavior.

**LRN-24 · Teaching others.** Teaching others effectively. From an explanation to teaching others effectively.

**LRN-25 · Lifelong learning design.** Designing a life organized around learning. From a course to designing a lifelong learning life.
