## Business & Entrepreneurship (BIZ)

**BIZ-01 · Opportunity discovery.** Seeing needs others overlook. From noticing a gap to finding opportunities others miss.

**BIZ-02 · Customer discovery.** Learning what people actually want. From a guess to running discovery that changes product direction.

**BIZ-03 · Market sizing.** Estimating how big an opportunity is. From a rough number to sizing markets with defensible methods.

**BIZ-04 · Value proposition.** Stating why someone should choose you. From a slogan to crafting value propositions that convert.

**BIZ-05 · Business models.** Designing how a venture captures value. From a simple model to designing business models that scale.

**BIZ-06 · Pricing strategy.** Pricing for value and sustainable growth. From a number to pricing for value and growth.

**BIZ-07 · Marketing fundamentals.** Getting noticed and chosen, honestly. From a first campaign to building marketing that compounds.

**BIZ-08 · Brand building.** Building a name that means something durable. From a logo to building brands with durable meaning.

**BIZ-09 · Sales systems.** Turning interest into revenue, repeatably. From one deal to building sales systems that scale.

**BIZ-10 · Growth loops.** Designing growth that feeds itself. From a channel to designing growth loops with healthy economics.

**BIZ-11 · Operations design.** Making the work run reliably at scale. From a process to designing operations for reliability and scale.

**BIZ-12 · Supply chain.** Moving goods despite disruption. From a vendor to building resilient supply chains.

**BIZ-13 · Hiring & culture.** Building the team and the values it runs on. From a first hire to hiring and building culture at scale.

**BIZ-14 · Fundraising.** Raising capital on terms that protect you. From a pitch to raising capital on strong terms.

**BIZ-15 · Financial modeling.** Building forecasts investors can trust. From a spreadsheet to building models investors trust.

**BIZ-16 · Legal formation.** Setting up a company correctly from day one. From registration to forming and structuring companies correctly.

**BIZ-17 · Product management.** Shipping products people love. From a feature to shipping products users love.

**BIZ-18 · Product analytics.** Using data to make product decisions. From a metric to using analytics to drive product decisions.

**BIZ-19 · Partnerships.** Building relationships that create leverage. From an introduction to building partnerships that create leverage.

**BIZ-20 · International expansion.** Growing across borders with local fit. From one market to expanding internationally with local fit.

**BIZ-21 · Turnaround management.** Rescuing a business in distress. From diagnosis to turning around distressed businesses.

**BIZ-22 · Mergers & acquisitions basics.** Evaluating and absorbing other companies. From due diligence to evaluating and integrating acquisitions.

**BIZ-23 · Governance.** Building the rules that earn trust. From a board to building governance that earns trust.

**BIZ-24 · Social enterprise.** Running a venture that creates real impact. From a mission to running a social enterprise with real impact.

**BIZ-25 · Scaling playbook.** Growing an organization without breaking it. From a growth spurt to scaling an organization without breaking it.
