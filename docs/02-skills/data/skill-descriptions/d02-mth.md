## Mathematics & Quantitative Reasoning (MTH)

**MTH-01 · Number sense.** An intuitive feel for magnitudes, units, and whether a number is right. From catching obvious errors to diagnosing subtle numerical mistakes instantly and teaching number sense to struggling adults.

**MTH-02 · Arithmetic fluency.** Fast, reliable mental computation as a background skill. From accurate basics to performing complex mental arithmetic reliably under pressure.

**MTH-03 · Fractions, ratios & percentages.** Proportional reasoning applied by instinct. From everyday percentages to applying proportional reasoning to novel policy and engineering problems.

**MTH-04 · Algebra.** The language of relationships between changing quantities. From solving equations to using algebraic modeling to crack open-ended problems across domains.

**MTH-05 · Geometry.** Shape, space, and the reasoning they demand. From basic figures to proving advanced geometric results and applying geometry in real design.

**MTH-06 · Statistics.** Reasoning from data while respecting uncertainty. From reading a mean to designing and critiquing analyses at research-publication level.

**MTH-07 · Probability.** Quantifying what can and cannot be known about the future. From basic chance to building probabilistic models for high-stakes decisions under uncertainty.

**MTH-08 · Data literacy.** Reading, questioning, and trusting numbers in the wild. From spotting a suspicious chart to detecting misleading claims and setting organizational data standards.

**MTH-09 · Financial numeracy.** Fluency with the arithmetic of money and time. From interest and budgeting to modeling complex personal and institutional finance with confidence.

**MTH-10 · Measurement & estimation.** Sizing the world quickly and accurately. From rough guesses to producing estimates within expert tolerance even in unfamiliar domains.

**MTH-11 · Logic & proofs.** The discipline of airtight reasoning. From basic logic to writing publishable proofs and catching subtle logical errors others miss.

**MTH-12 · Calculus.** Reasoning about continuous change and accumulation. From derivatives to applying calculus to novel physics, economics, and optimization problems.

**MTH-13 · Linear algebra.** The mathematics of vectors, matrices, and high-dimensional systems. From basic operations to building and analyzing large-scale systems with it.

**MTH-14 · Discrete math.** The mathematics of countable, finite structure. From counting and graphs to solving frontier combinatorial problems and teaching the field.

**MTH-15 · Optimization.** Finding the best solution under constraints. From simple trade-offs to formulating and solving large-scale optimization problems in production.

**MTH-16 · Modeling & simulation.** Building simplified worlds that predict reality. From a first model to validated models used for real policy or engineering decisions.

**MTH-17 · Spreadsheet modeling.** Turning a spreadsheet into an auditable decision tool. From basic formulas to auditable financial models used for major decisions.

**MTH-18 · Experiment design.** Structuring a test so its result actually means something. From a simple comparison to experiments that withstand peer review and scale.

**MTH-19 · Risk & uncertainty.** Measuring what could go wrong and how badly. From basic risk to quantifying and communicating tail risk to executives and regulators.

**MTH-20 · Graph literacy.** Reading charts with a trained eye. From interpreting a bar chart to exposing misleading visualizations and interpreting complex graphs.

**MTH-21 · Algorithmic thinking.** Solving problems as repeatable, correct procedures. From following steps to designing algorithms for novel problems with provable guarantees.

**MTH-22 · Cryptography math.** The mathematics of secrecy, integrity, and trust. From ciphers to contributing to cryptographic protocol design and analysis.

**MTH-23 · Game theory.** Reasoning about interacting, strategic decision-makers. From simple games to applying game theory to market design and strategic policy.

**MTH-24 · Quantitative research.** Producing rigorous, publishable numerical findings. From a clean analysis to leading quantitative research published in top venues.

**MTH-25 · Advanced mathematical writing.** Communicating mathematics with precision and clarity. From clear notation to writing exposition studied by specialists worldwide.
