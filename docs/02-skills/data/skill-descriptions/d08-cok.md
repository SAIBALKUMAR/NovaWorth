## Cooking & Nutrition (COK)

**COK-01 · Kitchen safety.** Working in a kitchen without injury or incident. From basic precautions to running a kitchen with zero serious safety incidents.

**COK-02 · Knife skills.** Safe, precise, efficient cutting. From basic grips to demonstrating professional knife speed and precision.

**COK-03 · Food hygiene.** Keeping food safe from contamination. From basic rules to implementing HACCP-level hygiene in a kitchen.

**COK-04 · Basic cooking methods.** The core techniques behind every recipe. From boiling and roasting to executing all core methods with consistent results.

**COK-05 · Meal planning.** Designing weeks of nourishing, affordable food. From a single plan to planning a month of varied, budgeted meals.

**COK-06 · Grocery budgeting.** Eating well on a constrained budget. From a list to feeding a family well on a constrained budget.

**COK-07 · Balanced plates.** Composing meals that cover real needs. From basic portions to designing balanced plates for diverse needs.

**COK-08 · Baking basics.** The precision craft of oven cooking. From a first loaf to baking consistently at home and teaching others.

**COK-09 · Sauces & seasoning.** The flavor logic beneath good cooking. From salt and acid to balancing flavor intuitively across cuisines.

**COK-10 · Batch cooking.** Cooking once, eating well all week. From doubling a recipe to building efficient batch-cooking systems.

**COK-11 · Cultural cuisines.** Cooking traditions with authenticity and respect. From one cuisine to cooking multiple cuisines with cultural fidelity.

**COK-12 · Fermentation.** Using microbes to transform food safely. From basics to fermenting safely and developing new products.

**COK-13 · Food preservation.** Extending food's life without losing safety. From freezing to preserving food safely at scale.

**COK-14 · Dietary restrictions.** Cooking well around what people can't eat. From substitutions to designing safe, satisfying menus for restrictions.

**COK-15 · Allergen safety.** Preventing allergic reactions in food. From labeling to preventing allergen incidents in professional settings.

**COK-16 · Nutrition label reading.** Decoding what's actually in food. From the panel to exposing misleading claims on labels.

**COK-17 · Sports nutrition.** Fueling performance and recovery. From basics to fueling athletes for performance and recovery.

**COK-18 · Cooking for families.** Feeding a household with variety and joy. From weeknight dinners to feeding families with variety, budget, and joy.

**COK-19 · Zero-waste cooking.** Using everything and wasting nothing. From scraps to running a near-zero-waste kitchen.

**COK-20 · Advanced plating.** Making food look as good as it tastes. From neat presentation to plating at fine-dining standard.

**COK-21 · Butchery & fish.** Breaking down proteins with skill and yield. From basic cuts to breaking down proteins with professional yield.

**COK-22 · Pastry.** The delicate craft of doughs and sweets. From a simple tart to producing pastry at professional standard.

**COK-23 · Restaurant operations.** Running a kitchen as a sustainable business. From service flow to running a restaurant's profit-and-loss and service.

**COK-24 · Food business.** Building a food venture people pay for. From an idea to launching and scaling a food business.

**COK-25 · Recipe development.** Creating recipes that work, every time, at scale. From adapting a recipe to developing recipes that work at scale.
