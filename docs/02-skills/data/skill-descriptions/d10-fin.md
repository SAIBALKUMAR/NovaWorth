## Finance & Economics (FIN)

**FIN-01 · Money basics.** The core literacy of earning, spending, and keeping money. From the fundamentals to teaching money fundamentals to adults and youth.

**FIN-02 · Budgeting.** Spending on purpose instead of by instinct. From a first budget to building budgets that survive real life.

**FIN-03 · Banking.** Using banks and accounts to your advantage. From opening an account to navigating banking systems and fees optimally.

**FIN-04 · Credit & debt.** Managing borrowing so it works for you. From a first card to repairing credit and managing debt strategically.

**FIN-05 · Saving.** Building reserves even on uneven income. From a first cushion to building saving systems for irregular income.

**FIN-06 · Investing fundamentals.** How risk and return actually work. From core concepts to explaining investing risk and return clearly.

**FIN-07 · Index investing.** The low-cost, long-horizon way to grow wealth. From fund basics to building low-cost portfolios for long horizons.

**FIN-08 · Taxes.** Understanding and filing what you owe. From a simple return to filing complex personal taxes accurately.

**FIN-09 · Insurance.** Buying protection that matches real risk. From policy basics to choosing insurance that matches real risk.

**FIN-10 · Retirement planning.** Planning income for decades of not working. From a first projection to building retirement plans under uncertainty.

**FIN-11 · Fraud awareness.** Spotting and stopping financial scams. From common scams to detecting and preventing financial fraud.

**FIN-12 · Housing finance.** Managing the biggest money decision most people make. From a lease to navigating mortgages and housing finance.

**FIN-13 · Consumer rights.** Knowing what you're owed and enforcing it. From returns to enforcing consumer rights effectively.

**FIN-14 · Pricing literacy.** Seeing through how prices manipulate you. From anchors to seeing through pricing tricks and anchors.

**FIN-15 · Inflation & interest.** How the value of money changes over time. From basic concepts to reasoning about inflation and interest in decisions.

**FIN-16 · Personal risk management.** Building a plan for what could go wrong. From an emergency fund to building a personal risk plan.

**FIN-17 · Small business finance.** Keeping a small business's money clean. From a ledger to running small-business finances cleanly.

**FIN-18 · Payroll & invoicing.** Paying people and getting paid correctly. From an invoice to running payroll and invoicing compliantly.

**FIN-19 · Accounting basics.** Recording money accurately and reconciling. From bookkeeping to keeping accurate books and reconciling.

**FIN-20 · Financial statements.** Reading a company's true financial story. From a balance sheet to reading and analyzing financial statements.

**FIN-21 · Valuation.** Figuring out what a business is worth. From basic multiples to valuing businesses with defensible methods.

**FIN-22 · Macro economics.** Reading the big economy and its signals. From GDP to interpreting macro data for decisions.

**FIN-23 · Market design.** Building markets where good behavior pays. From basic incentives to designing markets with good incentives.

**FIN-24 · Behavioral economics.** Understanding how people really decide. From biases to applying behavioral insights ethically.

**FIN-25 · Financial planning.** Turning goals into a complete money plan. From a single goal to building comprehensive financial plans.
