## Health & Body (HLT)

**HLT-01 · Sleep.** The foundation of health and cognition. From basic habits to designing sleep interventions that measurably improve health.

**HLT-02 · Nutrition basics.** Eating in a way your body can use. From balanced meals to building evidence-based nutrition plans for diverse populations.

**HLT-03 · Hydration & electrolytes.** Keeping water and salts in balance. From daily intake to preventing and managing hydration emergencies in sport and heat.

**HLT-04 · Exercise fundamentals.** Moving safely for lifelong health. From a first routine to coaching safe, effective exercise across ages and conditions.

**HLT-05 · Strength training.** Building muscle and bone with progressive load. From correct form to programming strength training at elite or clinical level.

**HLT-06 · Mobility & flexibility.** Maintaining full, pain-free range of motion. From basic stretching to restoring mobility in complex cases.

**HLT-07 · Cardio & endurance.** Conditioning the heart and lungs. From a first jog to designing endurance programs for performance and health.

**HLT-08 · Posture & ergonomics.** Aligning body and workspace to prevent injury. From awareness to fixing workplace ergonomics at organizational scale.

**HLT-09 · Preventive health.** Acting before illness arrives. From checkups to designing preventive health programs with measured outcomes.

**HLT-10 · First aid.** Confident response to injury and emergency. From basics to handling emergencies calmly and training first responders.

**HLT-11 · CPR & AED.** Restarting heart and breath in a crisis. From the technique to leading CPR response and training whole communities.

**HLT-12 · Infection control.** Stopping the spread of disease. From hand hygiene to designing infection-control protocols for high-risk settings.

**HLT-13 · Medication literacy.** Understanding what you take and why. From reading a label to interpreting medication regimens and preventing errors.

**HLT-14 · Chronic condition management.** Living well with long-term illness. From self-care basics to coaching complex chronic care alongside clinicians.

**HLT-15 · Pain management.** Managing pain with evidence, within scope. From basic strategies to applying evidence-based pain management appropriately.

**HLT-16 · Sexual health.** Accurate, nonjudgmental knowledge of sexual wellbeing. From the facts to providing accurate, nonjudgmental sexual health guidance.

**HLT-17 · Reproductive health.** Understanding fertility, pregnancy, and care. From the basics to supporting reproductive health decisions with clinical literacy.

**HLT-18 · Aging & elder care.** Supporting dignity and safety in later life. From awareness to designing elder-care plans that preserve dignity and safety.

**HLT-19 · Disability support.** Person-centered care that honors autonomy. From communication basics to providing person-centered disability support at expert level.

**HLT-20 · Physical rehabilitation.** Rebuilding function after injury or illness. From guided exercise to guiding rehabilitation programs alongside clinicians.

**HLT-21 · Emergency preparedness.** Readiness before disaster strikes. From a go-bag to building household and community emergency plans.

**HLT-22 · Health advocacy.** Navigating systems for a patient's benefit. From self-advocacy to navigating health systems and advocating for complex patients.

**HLT-23 · Lab & vital interpretation.** Reading the numbers your body reports. From normal ranges to interpreting common labs and vitals within scope.

**HLT-24 · Surgery literacy.** Understanding what surgery involves. From informed consent to understanding surgical pathways and supporting recovery.

**HLT-25 · Public health.** Health at the population scale. From basic epidemiology to designing public-health interventions at population scale.
