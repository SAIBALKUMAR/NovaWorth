## Mental Health & Mind (MND)

**MND-01 · Emotional awareness.** Knowing and naming what you feel. From basic labels to teaching emotional granularity and regulation at scale.

**MND-02 · Stress management.** Keeping pressure from becoming harm. From coping tools to designing stress-reduction programs with measured outcomes.

**MND-03 · Anxiety coping.** Managing worry with evidence-based tools. From breathing basics to coaching evidence-based anxiety coping within scope.

**MND-04 · Low-mood coping.** Supporting a low mood safely. From self-care to supporting low mood safely and knowing when to escalate.

**MND-05 · Sleep hygiene for mind.** Protecting the sleep that protects the mind. From habits to designing sleep-mind interventions for clinical-adjacent populations.

**MND-06 · Mindfulness.** Paying attention on purpose, without judgment. From brief practice to teaching mindfulness with fidelity and safety.

**MND-07 · Cognitive reframing.** Changing the thoughts that shape the feeling. From noticing distortions to coaching reframing that holds up under stress.

**MND-08 · Trauma-informed basics.** Working in ways that don't re-harm. From core principles to applying trauma-informed practice across organizations.

**MND-09 · Grief support.** Accompanying people through loss. From presence to supporting grieving people with skill and boundaries.

**MND-10 · Addiction awareness.** Recognizing addiction and pathways to care. From the signs to recognizing addiction and connecting people to care.

**MND-11 · Boundaries.** Protecting yourself while staying connected. From saying no to teaching boundaries in high-conflict systems.

**MND-12 · Self-compassion.** Treating yourself with the kindness you'd offer a friend. From gentle self-talk to building practices that reduce burnout.

**MND-13 · Resilience.** Recovering and adapting without self-blame. From coping to designing resilience programs that do not blame individuals.

**MND-14 · Attention management.** Directing focus on purpose. From reducing distraction to designing attention systems for knowledge work.

**MND-15 · Digital wellbeing.** A healthier relationship with screens. From awareness to helping people rebuild healthy digital habits.

**MND-16 · Motivation design.** Building systems that support drive and autonomy. From intrinsic motivation to designing systems that support autonomy.

**MND-17 · Habit formation.** Making good behavior automatic. From one habit to building habit systems that survive disruption.

**MND-18 · Therapy literacy.** Understanding the options and evidence for care. From the basics to navigating therapy options and evidence.

**MND-19 · Peer support.** Helping others from shared experience, safely. From listening to providing peer support with strong boundaries and supervision.

**MND-20 · Crisis support.** Responding to a mental-health crisis safely. From recognition to responding safely and connecting to emergency care.

**MND-21 · Burnout prevention.** Stopping exhaustion before it breaks people. From warning signs to diagnosing and fixing organizational burnout drivers.

**MND-22 · Psychological safety.** Making it safe to speak and fail. From trust to building psychologically safe teams with measurable change.

**MND-23 · Neurodiversity awareness.** Design that includes different minds. From understanding to designing environments that support neurodivergent people.

**MND-24 · Couples communication.** The skills that keep a partnership healthy. From active listening to teaching couples communication within scope.

**MND-25 · Mental health first aid.** First response to a mental-health emergency. From core steps to responding to emergencies and training others.
