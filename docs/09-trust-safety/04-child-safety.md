# Child Safety

## 1. Purpose

Minors can learn and earn, but they need stronger protections: guardian consent, spending limits, content controls, privacy, and safeguarding. This document defines the rules.

**Minimum age:** 13 with guardian consent; 16+ standalone (region-dependent). Under-13 access is not supported in v1.

---

## 2. Guardian model

### 2.1 Setup
1. Guardian verifies identity.
2. Guardian consents to data processing.
3. Guardian creates/links the minor account.
4. Guardian sets limits and controls.
5. Minor completes onboarding with age-appropriate content.

### 2.2 Guardian controls
| Control | Options |
|---|---|
| Screen time | Daily/weekly limits |
| Spending | Allow/deny, caps, approvals |
| Social | Guilds off/on, private only |
| Content | Age ratings, high-risk skills off |
| Data | Minimal collection |
| Notifications | Guardian-controlled |

### 2.3 Guardian visibility
- Progress summary.
- Pass/fail (not raw responses).
- Spending.
- Safety alerts.
- No private conversations.

---

## 3. Data minimization

- Collect only what is needed.
- No work history for minors (unless relevant).
- No precise location.
- No biometrics unless required for high-stakes (and consented).
- No behavioral advertising.
- No sale of data.

---

## 4. Content safety

| Age | Allowed | Blocked |
|---|---|---|
| 13–15 | Foundational skills, supervised physical | High-risk, clinical, financial advice |
| 16–17 | Most skills with warnings | Restricted categories |
| 18+ | All | Illegal content |

- Age ratings on skills.
- Safety briefs.
- No adult content.
- No gambling.
- No weapons training.

---

## 5. Social safety

- Private by default.
- No direct messages from adults unless guardian-approved mentor.
- No public profile by default.
- No location sharing.
- Report/block everywhere.
- Human moderation.
- Safeguarding escalation.

---

## 6. Spending

- Guardian approval for all redemptions.
- No peer transfers.
- No cash-out.
- Category restrictions.
- Monthly caps.
- Receipts visible to guardian.

---

## 7. Safeguarding

### 7.1 Red flags
- Self-harm.
- Abuse.
- Exploitation.
- Grooming.
- Threats.
- Severe distress.

### 7.2 Response
1. Immediate safety resources.
2. Notify guardian (unless abuse by guardian).
3. Escalate to safety team.
4. Report to authorities where required.
5. Document.

### 7.3 Staff
- Background checks.
- Training.
- No private contact with minors.
- Two-adult rule for live sessions.

---

## 8. Compliance

- COPPA (US).
- GDPR-K (EU).
- Age-appropriate design codes.
- Local child protection laws.
- App store age ratings.
- School/guardian consent where required.

---

## 9. Requirements

- `FR-TNS-401`: Minors require guardian consent.
- `FR-TNS-402`: Guardian controls are enforced.
- `FR-TNS-403`: Minors are private by default.
- `FR-TNS-404`: No peer transfers for minors.
- `FR-TNS-405`: Spending requires approval.
- `FR-TNS-406`: Content is age-rated.
- `FR-TNS-407`: High-risk skills are restricted.
- `FR-TNS-408`: Safeguarding escalation exists.
- `FR-TNS-409`: Staff are trained and checked.
- `FR-TNS-410`: Data collection is minimized.
- `NFR-TNS-401`: Safety reports acknowledged <1h.
- `NFR-TNS-402`: Guardian controls apply immediately.
