# Practical Work, Artifacts & Portfolios

## 1. Purpose

At higher levels, proof must be a thing the learner made or did: code, a dish, a repair, a lesson, a negotiation, a system, a business. This document defines how practical work is designed, submitted, verified, and scored.

---

## 2. Artifact types

| Type | Examples | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Code | Repo, service, script | Tests, review, provenance |
| Document | Plan, policy, essay | Rubric, similarity, sources |
| Spreadsheet | Model, forecast | Formula audit, scenario test |
| Design | UI, poster, 3D model | Rubric, critique |
| Media | Video, podcast, article | Rubric, fact-check |
| Physical | Dish, repair, build | Video, photos, witness |
| Performance | Talk, role-play, sport | Recording, live assessor |
| Business | Launch, campaign, P&L | Metrics, partner verification |
| Real-world | Community project, work outcome | Partner verification |

---

## 3. Submission

### 3.1 Requirements
- Artifact file or link.
- Description of what the learner did.
- Tools and AI assistance declared.
- Date and version.
- Evidence of process (drafts, commits, photos).
- Self-assessment against the rubric.

### 3.2 Provenance
- File hash.
- Version history.
- Commit graph for code.
- Screen recording for practical tasks.
- Photo/video metadata.
- AI-use declaration.
- Witness/partner statement where relevant.

### 3.3 Storage
- Encrypted at rest.
- Access-controlled.
- Retained for credential lifetime.
- Exportable by learner.
- Deletable after credential expiry (unless legal hold).

## 4. Rubric scoring

### 4.1 Criteria
| Criterion | What it tests |
|---|---|
| Correctness | Does it work / is it accurate? |
| Method | Is the approach sound? |
| Judgment | Are tradeoffs and edge cases handled? |
| Communication | Is it clear and usable? |
| Ethics/safety | Does it avoid harm? |

### 4.2 Scale
1. Not yet
2. Developing
3. Proficient
4. Exemplary

### 4.3 Rules
- Critical criteria have a floor of 2.
- Safety has a floor of 3 for high-risk.
- No compensation across critical criteria.
- Anchors exist for each score.
- Two graders for high stakes.

## 5. Peer review

### 5.1 When
- Bands 3–6: optional peer feedback.
- Bands 7+: peer review required before panel.
- Peer review is never the sole score at 80+.

### 5.2 Process
1. Assign 2–3 peers with relevant level.
2. Blind review.
3. Rubric scores + written feedback.
4. Author responds.
5. Discrepancies escalate to a human grader.

### 5.3 Quality
- Reviewer calibration.
- Reviewer reputation.
- Spot checks.
- Collusion detection.
- Removal for poor reviews.

---

## 6. Expert panels

### 6.1 When
- Level 80+ artifacts.
- Level 90–100 credentials.
- Frontier challenges.
- Appeals.

### 6.2 Composition
- 3–5 experts.
- At least one external.
- Domain coverage.
- Conflict-of-interest declaration.
- Calibration training.

### 6.3 Process
- Artifact packet 48h prior.
- 60–90 minute defense.
- Live task or simulation.
- Deliberation.
- Written decision with evidence.

---

## 7. Portfolio

### 7.1 Structure
- 3–5 artifacts.
- Each with context, role, outcome, evidence.
- A narrative of growth.
- External references.
- Public/private toggle.

### 7.2 Uses
- Level 90–100 panel.
- Employer verification.
- Guild leadership.
- Mentoring.
- Bounties.

### 7.3 Quality
- Authentic work.
- Clear attribution.
- Measured outcomes.
- Reflection on failure.
- No AI-generated portfolio without disclosure.

---

## 8. Real-world verification

| Evidence | Verification |
|---|---|
| Work outcome | Manager/client statement |
| Community project | Partner letter + photos |
| Business result | Financials + partner |
| Teaching | Learner outcomes |
| Safety | Incident-free record |
| Research | Publication/peer review |

Verification is required for level 90+ and for impact bounties.

---

## 9. Anti-gaming

- Provenance checks.
- Process evidence.
- Oral defense.
- Randomized tasks.
- Similarity detection.
- AI-use declaration.
- Panel review.
- Post-hoc audits.

---

## 10. Accessibility

- Alternative artifact formats.
- Oral instead of written.
- Extended time.
- Assistive technology.
- Human support.
- No vision-only evidence.

---

## 11. Requirements

- `FR-ASM-501`: Every practical task has a rubric.
- `FR-ASM-502`: Every artifact has provenance.
- `FR-ASM-503`: Every high-stakes artifact has two graders or a panel.
- `FR-ASM-504`: Peer review is calibrated.
- `FR-ASM-505`: Portfolios are exportable.
- `FR-ASM-506`: Real-world claims are verified.
- `FR-ASM-507`: AI assistance is declared.
- `FR-ASM-508`: Artifacts are stored securely.
- `FR-ASM-509`: Accessibility alternatives exist.
- `FR-ASM-510`: Plagiarism is detected and appealable.
- `NFR-ASM-501`: Upload resumes after disconnect.
- `NFR-ASM-502`: Artifact preview <3 s.
