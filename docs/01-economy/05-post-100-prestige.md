# Post-100 Prestige

## 1. Purpose

Level 100 is not the end. It is the point where the product stops asking "can you do this?" and starts asking "what will you do with it?"

Post-100 is designed to:
- preserve the meaning of level 100,
- keep the most capable learners engaged for decades,
- generate real-world value,
- avoid infinite point farming,
- create a path from learner to master, examiner, mentor, and builder.

---

## 2. Prestige levels

After level 100, learners enter **Prestige (P1, P2, …)**. Prestige is earned through **frontier challenges**, not by repeating content.

### 2.1 Rewards

| Prestige level | EC (relative to L100) | XP | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 50% | 10,000 | Frontier challenges, mentor eligibility |
| P2 | 31% | 14,100 | Constellation missions |
| P3 | 23% | 17,300 | Peer review privileges |
| P5 | 16% | 22,400 | Examiner shadowing |
| P10 | 10% | 31,600 | Frontier Fellow status |
| P25 | 5% | 50,000 | Guild leadership |
| P50 | 3% | 70,700 | Grandmaster Circle |
| P100 | 2% | 100,000 | Legend |

Formulas:
```
EC(Pn) = EC(100) × 0.5 / n^0.7
XP(Pn) = 10,000 × n^0.5
```

### 2.2 Why diminishing EC
- The economy cannot fund infinite high-level payouts.
- Status, access, and impact become the dominant rewards.
- Diminishing returns prevent a small elite from extracting the entire sponsor pool.
- Prestige remains prestigious because it is hard, not because it is lucrative.

## 3. Frontier challenge types

| Type | Description | Evidence | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live incident** | Timed outage, security breach, kitchen rush, medical emergency drill | Recording, logs, decisions, after-action report | Expert panel |
| **Open problem** | A real unsolved problem from a partner | Artifact, method, result | Panel + partner |
| **Build challenge** | Ship a working system under constraints | Code, deployment, metrics | Automated + human |
| **Research brief** | Investigate a question with rigor | Paper, data, reproducibility | Peer review |
| **Constellation** | Combine 3+ skills to solve a cross-domain problem | Integrated artifact | Multi-expert panel |
| **Teach-back** | Teach a level 60–80 concept to others | Recorded lesson, learner outcomes | Peer + expert |
| **Bounty** | Paid real-world task from an employer/partner | Verified outcome | Partner + platform |
| **Adversarial** | Red-team a system, recipe, argument, or plan | Findings, fixes | Expert panel |

## 4. Constellations

A **constellation** is a cross-skill mission that cannot be solved by one skill alone. It is the signature post-100 experience.

### 4.1 Examples

| Constellation | Skills | Mission |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-native hospital** | Health, AI, operations, communication | Design and simulate a clinic with agentic workflows |
| **Resilient city** | Civics, systems, energy, logistics | Respond to a cascading infrastructure failure |
| **Food system** | Cooking, supply chain, nutrition, business | Launch a zero-waste meal service |
| **Learning company** | Learning science, AI, business, design | Build a product that teaches a hard skill |
| **Crisis newsroom** | Media, law, communication, data | Publish verified coverage during a breaking event |
| **Space habitat** | Science, engineering, health, survival | Plan a 90-day mission with constrained resources |

### 4.2 Constellation requirements
- At least 3 skills, each at level 60+ (or 80+ for the lead skill).
- A real constraint: budget, time, safety, regulation, or conflicting stakeholders.
- A multi-artifact deliverable.
- A panel with at least one expert from each domain.
- A public or partner-facing outcome where possible.

### 4.3 Scoring
Constellations award:
- Prestige progress (primary).
- XP (large).
- EC (capped; funded by sponsor/bounty).
- Reputation (on successful review).
- A constellation badge on the skill wallet.

## 5. Bounties and real-world work

### 5.1 Bounty types
- **Micro-bounty:** 1–10 hours, e.g., fix a bug, review a menu, translate a guide.
- **Project bounty:** 1–6 weeks, e.g., build an internal tool, design a curriculum.
- **Impact bounty:** 1–6 months, e.g., improve a clinic workflow, reduce food waste.

### 5.2 Rules
- Bounties are posted by verified partners.
- Scope, payment, and acceptance criteria are explicit.
- Learners must have the relevant level (usually 50+).
- Payment is in EC (and optionally fiat by the partner).
- Disputes go through a structured review.
- No bounty may require unpaid speculative work.
- No bounty may be used to replace employees unlawfully.

### 5.3 Anti-exploitation
- Minimum EC per hour floor.
- Maximum revision rounds.
- Clear IP assignment.
- Partner rating and removal for bad behavior.

## 6. Mentoring and examining

### 6.1 Mentor
- Eligibility: level 60+ in the skill, reputation ≥ threshold, training completed.
- Duties: review practice, run office hours, guide projects.
- Rewards: EC per verified session, reputation, prestige progress.
- Quality: learner ratings, outcome deltas, spot checks.

### 6.2 Examiner
- Eligibility: level 90+ or equivalent expert credential, panel training, conflict-of-interest declaration.
- Duties: score level 90–100 artifacts, frontier challenges, appeals.
- Rewards: per-review fee, prestige, council eligibility.
- Quality: calibration sessions, agreement statistics, audits.

### 6.3 Teaching as mastery
Teaching is one of the strongest signals of understanding. A learner who can teach a concept to novices and improve their outcomes earns mastery credit — but only if the learners actually improve.

---

## 7. Reputation and governance

Prestige unlocks:
- Peer review privileges.
- Guild leadership.
- Examiner shadowing.
- Economy Council candidacy (Phase 5).
- Invitation-only research and partner projects.
- Public "Frontier Fellow" profile.

Reputation is separate from prestige:
- Prestige = what you have achieved.
- Reputation = how reliably you contribute.
- A high-prestige learner with poor integrity loses reputation and access.

---

## 8. Anti-farming and integrity

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Repeating easy frontier tasks | Task novelty requirement; partner-defined problems |
| Collusion on bounties | Provenance, contribution logs, partner verification |
| AI-generated artifacts | Oral defense, process evidence, reproducibility |
| Panel capture | Rotating panels, conflict checks, appeals |
| Status inflation | Fixed prestige thresholds; no purchasable prestige |
| Burnout | Voluntary participation; no decay of level 100 |

## 9. Learner-facing experience

### 9.1 After level 100
The learner sees:
- "You have mastered the ladder. Now choose your frontier."
- A menu of frontier challenges, constellations, bounties, and mentoring.
- A prestige path with clear requirements.
- An invitation to join the Guild of Masters.

### 9.2 Failure
Frontier failure is expected. It yields:
- A detailed after-action review.
- Partial XP for legitimate effort.
- No EC unless a deliverable is accepted.
- A path to retry or choose a different challenge.

### 9.3 Recognition
- Prestige badge on profile and wallet.
- Public case study (with consent).
- Invitation to annual Frontier Assembly.
- Eligibility for research grants and partner projects.

## 10. Requirements

- `FR-PRS-001`: Level 100 requires panel review and verified impact.
- `FR-PRS-002`: Prestige is earned only through approved frontier activity.
- `FR-PRS-003`: Prestige cannot be purchased or transferred.
- `FR-PRS-004`: EC for prestige follows the diminishing formula.
- `FR-PRS-005`: Constellations require 3+ skills and multi-expert review.
- `FR-PRS-006`: Bounties have explicit scope, pay, and acceptance criteria.
- `FR-PRS-007`: Mentors and examiners are trained, rated, and audited.
- `FR-PRS-008`: Learners can stop at any prestige level without losing status.
- `FR-PRS-009`: Frontier work has an after-action review.
- `FR-PRS-010`: Prestige activity is visible in the skill wallet.
