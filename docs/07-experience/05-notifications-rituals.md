# Notifications & Rituals

## 1. Purpose

Rituals create rhythm; notifications support it. Both must respect attention and wellbeing. The product should feel like a gym with a good coach, not a slot machine.

**Rule:** no dark patterns, no fake urgency, no streak shaming.

---

## 2. Rituals

### 2.1 Daily
| Ritual | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Morning review | 2–5 min | Retrieval, momentum |
| Focus session | 25 min | Deep practice |
| Evening reflection | 2 min | Consolidation |

### 2.2 Weekly
- Week review: progress, wins, next goal.
- Guild check-in.
- One applied task.
- Rest day encouraged.

### 2.3 Monthly
- Skill progress review.
- Wallet and savings goal.
- Path check-in.
- Feedback survey (optional).

### 2.4 Seasonal (12 weeks)
- League reset.
- Prestige challenge.
- Cohort event.
- Economy report.

---

## 3. Notifications

### 3.1 Categories
| Category | Default | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Learning reminder | On | 1/day |
| Review due | On | 1/day |
| Guild | On | 3/week |
| Mentor | On | 2/week |
| Wallet/award | On | Immediate |
| Marketplace | Off | 1/week |
| Marketing | Off | 1/week |
| Safety | Always | As needed |

### 3.2 Rules
- Max 1 learning notification/day.
- Quiet hours respected.
- Time zone aware.
- Easy unsubscribe per category.
- No fake urgency.
- No streak loss threats.
- No "you're falling behind" shaming.
- Deep link to the exact action.

### 3.3 Channels
- In-app.
- Push.
- Email.
- SMS (safety only).
- Calendar (opt-in).

---

## 4. Momentum (not streaks)

### 4.1 Definition
- 3 sessions/week = momentum.
- No loss for missing a day.
- Rest is part of the plan.
- Momentum badge, not a fragile streak.

### 4.2 Why
- Streaks create anxiety and churn.
- Life happens; the product should not punish it.
- Consistency matters more than perfection.

---

## 5. Wellbeing

### 5.1 Guardrails
- Daily counted effort cap (6h soft).
- Break nudges after 90 minutes.
- Late-night study warnings.
- Burnout self-check monthly.
- Easy pause.
- No infinite scroll.

### 5.2 Signals
- Session length.
- Error spikes.
- Late-night use.
- Self-reported fatigue.
- Missed basics (sleep, meals).

### 5.3 Response
- Suggest a break.
- Offer a 2-minute review.
- Remind of rest.
- Never penalize stopping.

---

## 6. Celebration

- Level up: confetti optional, dismissible.
- Credential: share card.
- Prestige: profile badge.
- Guild: collective celebration.
- No celebration for spending EC.

---

- `FR-SOC-202`: Quiet hours are respected.

- `FR-SOC-205`: Marketing is opt-in.
- `FR-SOC-206`: Wellbeing nudges exist.
- `FR-SOC-207`: Daily effort cap is enforced.
- `FR-SOC-208`: Rest is never penalized.
- `FR-SOC-209`: Safety notifications cannot be disabled.
- `FR-SOC-210`: Celebration is dismissible.
- `NFR-SOC-201`: Push delivery <5 s.
