# Social, Guilds & Mentorship

## 1. Purpose

Learning is lonely at the top and at the bottom. Social systems should:
- make progress visible without shaming,
- connect learners at similar levels,
- enable peer review and mentorship,
- create belonging without toxicity,
- protect minors and vulnerable learners.

**Rule:** social features never gate learning or earning.

---

## 2. Guilds

### 2.1 What is a guild?
A small group (5–50) organized around a skill, domain, or goal.

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Skill guild | "Go backend builders" |
| Domain guild | "Health & care" |
| Cohort guild | Sponsor cohort |
| Study guild | 5 people, same level |
| Master guild | Level 80+ |

### 2.2 Guild features
- Shared goal and schedule.
- Discussion (threaded, moderated).
- Peer review.
- Study sessions.
- Leaderboard (opt-in).
- Mentorship.
- Events.

### 2.3 Guild roles
| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| Leader | Manage members, events |
| Mentor | Review, office hours |
| Member | Participate |
| Moderator | Safety |

### 2.4 Creation
- Level 20+ to create.
- Name and description review.
- No hateful or spam names.
- Minors cannot create public guilds.

---

## 3. Leagues

### 3.1 Structure
- 12-week seasons.
- Leagues by level band and activity.
- Opt-in.
- Reset each season.
- Mastery never resets.

### 3.2 Scoring
- Verified levels passed.
- Quality-adjusted.
- No points for logins.
- No pay-to-win.
- Small guild bonus for peer review.

### 3.3 Anti-toxicity
- No global "loser" board.
- No public failure scores.
- No shaming notifications.
- Report/mute/block.

---

## 4. Mentorship

### 4.1 Mentor eligibility
- Level 60+ in the skill.
- Reputation threshold.
- Training completed.
- Background check for minors (where required).

### 4.2 Matching
- Skill and level.
- Language.
- Availability.
- Goals.
- Accessibility.
- No demographic targeting.

### 4.3 Sessions
- 25–50 minutes.
- Agenda and outcome.
- Notes shared with learner.
- Rating and feedback.
- Safeguarding rules for minors.

### 4.4 Compensation
- EC per verified session.
- Reputation.
- Prestige progress.
- Quality bonuses.

---

## 5. Peer review

- Blind review.
- Rubric-based.
- 2–3 reviewers.
- Calibration.
- Reputation impact.
- Collusion detection.
- Appeal to human grader.

---

## 6. Community standards

### 6.1 Allowed
- Encouragement.
- Specific feedback.
- Questions.
- Sharing work.
- Celebrating progress.

### 6.2 Not allowed
- Harassment, hate, threats.
- Spam, scams, cheating.
- Sharing gate answers.
- Impersonation.
- Sexual content.
- Self-harm content.
- Doxxing.
- Discriminatory language.

### 6.3 Enforcement
| Action | Response |
|---|---|
| Minor | Warning |
| Repeated | Mute/restrict |
| Severe | Ban |
| Illegal | Report to authorities |

Appeals available.

---

## 7. Privacy

- Real name optional.
- Profile visibility controls.
- No location sharing by default.
- No contact info in public.
- Minors: private by default.
- Guild membership private by default.

---

## 8. Requirements

- `FR-SOC-001`: Guilds are optional.
- `FR-SOC-002`: Guild creation requires level 20+.
- `FR-SOC-003`: Leagues are opt-in and seasonal.
- `FR-SOC-004`: No global failure leaderboard.
- `FR-SOC-005`: Mentors are trained and rated.
- `FR-SOC-006`: Peer review is blind and calibrated.
- `FR-SOC-007`: Community standards are enforced.
- `FR-SOC-008`: Minors are private by default.
- `FR-SOC-009`: Report/mute/block exist everywhere.
- `FR-SOC-010`: Social features never gate earning.
- `NFR-SOC-001`: Report response <24h.
- `NFR-SOC-002`: Moderation coverage 24/7 for public spaces.
